## Key idea

Two of the best-supported study strategies are:

1. **Spacing**: distribute study sessions over time instead of cramming.
2. **Retrieval practice**: test yourself rather than re-reading.

## Why they work

Forgetting is steepest soon after learning. Each successful, effortful recall resets and flattens the curve, so reviews can be spaced further apart.
