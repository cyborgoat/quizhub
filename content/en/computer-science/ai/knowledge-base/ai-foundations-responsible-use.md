## Think in systems, not labels

An AI application includes more than a model: data, prompts, tools, interfaces, policies, and people all shape its behavior. A model's output is an estimate produced under learned patterns, not a guarantee of truth or a substitute for defining the task and acceptable error.

Evaluate the whole system against realistic cases, including failures and affected groups. Retrieval can provide relevant source material, but grounding does not guarantee that a generated answer accurately uses it. Check citations and outputs where correctness matters.

## Match oversight to impact

Consider privacy, bias, security, and the consequences of mistakes before deployment. Use human review where decisions are consequential, make uncertainty and limitations clear, and provide escalation paths. Monitoring should look for distribution changes and emerging harms, not only average model quality.
