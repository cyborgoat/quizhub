## Separate planned and exploratory analysis

Confirmatory analyses evaluate questions and methods specified in advance; exploratory analyses investigate patterns suggested by the data. Both can contribute to knowledge, but they answer different questions. Preregistration and analysis plans make prior commitments visible. If the plan changes, document what changed, why, and whether the change followed access to outcome data.

Reproducibility depends on a clear record of data processing, code, software, and analytic decisions. Share materials when ethically and legally appropriate, and explain restrictions when data cannot be public. Reproducible code does not guarantee sound measurement or a correct interpretation; it makes the computational path inspectable.

## Report estimates with uncertainty

Report effect estimates and uncertainty, not only thresholded significance decisions. Missing data, multiple comparisons, and flexible analytic choices can affect conclusions. Sensitivity analyses show whether results depend on plausible assumptions; transparent reporting lets readers judge their robustness.
