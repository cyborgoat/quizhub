## Design work for attention and coordination

Software development depends on focused reasoning and frequent collaboration. Interruptions and unclear priorities add coordination costs, so teams should make ownership, interfaces, and decision records easy to find. Small, reviewable changes and automated checks help expose errors while keeping feedback manageable.

Psychological safety supports asking questions, reporting uncertainty, and surfacing defects early. It is compatible with high standards: review the work carefully while treating people respectfully. A blameless incident review examines system conditions and recovery opportunities rather than stopping at an individual's mistake.

## Improve the work system

Estimate and plan with uncertainty visible; estimates are not promises. Use retrospectives and operational evidence to adjust workload, handoffs, and support. Avoid incentives that reward speed alone when reliability, maintainability, and user impact also matter. Make room for learning, documentation, and sustainable recovery after demanding work.
