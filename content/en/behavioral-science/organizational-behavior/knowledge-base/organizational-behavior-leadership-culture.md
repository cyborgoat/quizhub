## Leadership is shaped by context

Leadership involves influencing coordinated work, not simply holding a senior title. Effective approaches depend on the task, team expertise, and constraints. Clear expectations and support can help people coordinate; inviting input is especially useful when team members hold relevant information or will carry out the decision.

Organizational culture is reflected in shared assumptions, routines, and signals about what is rewarded or discouraged. A stated value matters less than repeated decisions and behavior. Psychological safety means people can raise concerns or admit mistakes without undue interpersonal punishment; it does not mean avoiding accountability or standards.

## Change the system, not just the slogan

To improve a team climate, identify the practices that shape daily behavior: who gets heard, how errors are handled, how resources are allocated, and how success is measured. Sustainable change needs consistent actions and feedback, not only a new mission statement.
