## Key idea

We judge how likely something is by how easily examples come to mind. Vivid, recent or emotional events are easy to recall, so they feel more common than they are.

## Example

Heavy media coverage of plane crashes makes flying feel riskier than driving, although driving causes far more deaths per mile travelled.
